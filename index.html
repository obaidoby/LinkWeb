Summary

Environmental engineer with a Ph.D. in Civil Engineering (Water Resources & Environmental Engineering) from Yeungnam University, South Korea. My doctoral work designed ZnS- and BiVO₄-based photocatalysts and a scalable reactor to remove pharmaceutical contaminants and inactivate multidrug-resistant bacteria in water. Earlier, I taught environmental engineering as an Assistant Professor and assessed full-scale effluent treatment plants. Today I combine water-treatment science with AI tools to build practical environmental solutions. Author of 16 publications.
